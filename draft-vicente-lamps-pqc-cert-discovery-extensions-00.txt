Post-Quantum and Multi-Tenant Extensions to X.509 Certificate Discovery
          draft-vicente-lamps-pqc-cert-discovery-extensions-00

Abstract

   This document extends the X.509 certificate discovery mechanism with
   post-quantum cryptographic (PQC) algorithm capability advertisement,
   hybrid-pair locator records, and tenant-isolated discovery
   namespaces.  The extensions enable a multi-tenant Certification
   Authority (CA) to publish per-tenant PQC algorithm sets without
   leaking neighbor-tenant inventory.

   The extensions are backward-compatible with the base certificate
   discovery mechanism.  Tenant carve-out implementation parameters are
   deployment-specific and are not disclosed in this Internet-Draft.

1.  Introduction

   Certificate discovery enables relying parties to locate certificates
   by subject, issuer, or algorithm capability.  Multi-tenant CAs need
   to expose PQC capability per tenant without exposing the aggregate
   inventory of all tenants.  This document defines the necessary
   discovery extensions.

2.  Terminology

   The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "SHOULD", "MAY" in this document
   are to be interpreted as described in BCP 14 [RFC2119] [RFC8174]
   when, and only when, they appear in all capitals, as shown here.

3.  PQC Capability Advertisement

   Discovery responders MAY advertise a pqcCapability set containing
   supported ML-KEM, ML-DSA, and SLH-DSA parameter sets, expressed as
   OBJECT IDENTIFIERs.

4.  Hybrid-Pair Locator Records

   A hybridPairLocator record binds a classical certificate to its post-
   quantum companion certificate or to a composite certificate chain.
   Relying parties MAY use the locator to fetch the companion artifact
   in a single round trip.

5.  Tenant-Isolated Namespaces

   Discovery responders compliant with this document MUST scope
   responses to the requesting tenant context.  The concrete carve-out
   parameters and namespace partition implementation are deployment-
   specific and are not disclosed in this Internet-Draft.

6.  Security Considerations

   PQC capability and hybrid-pair locators are sensitive metadata.
   Responders SHOULD authenticate requesters and rate-limit cross-tenant
   discovery probes.

7.  IANA Considerations

   IANA is requested to allocate object identifiers under the
   appropriate PKIX arc for pqcCapability and hybridPairLocator
   attributes.
